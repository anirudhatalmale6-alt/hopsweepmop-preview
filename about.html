Our story

Dubai's most reliable cleaning company since 2018

Established in December 2018, Hop Sweep Mop Cleaning Services LLC was built on one simple idea: cleaning you can actually trust. We provide customized cleaning tailored to your everyday needs, so you can focus on what truly matters to you.

Our business grows through referrals from highly satisfied clients. Thanks to our dedicated team, we consistently deliver exceptional results and maintain a high customer retention rate — because we treat every home and business as if it were our own.

Our management personally interviews and selects every candidate based on thorough background checks, experience and passion for cleaning. We strictly do not hire part-time maids or third-party cleaners, and all our staff are on company sponsorship — so we take full responsibility for our team.

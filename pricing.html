Pricing questions

Good to know

Is the price per cleaner or per job?

Rates are per cleaner, per hour. So two cleaners for three hours would be 6 cleaner-hours. We'll always confirm the estimate before we start.

What's the difference between "with" and "without" materials?

"With materials" means our team brings all equipment and eco-friendly supplies — nothing for you to prepare. "Without materials" is slightly cheaper and means you provide the equipment and products.

Do you have a minimum?

Yes — a 3-hour minimum per cleaner. Beyond 4 hours the hourly rate drops.

How do I pay?

We'll confirm accepted payment methods when we confirm your booking. There's no payment required to request a booking online.
